# Text doubts found while writing meanings (check against a printed critical edition)

- 5.132, speaker line "विदुरोवाच": the adhyayas around it are Vidula's speech to her son (131, 133, 134 read "मातोवाच" / "पुत्र उवाच"), so this is probably "विदुलोवाच" (र for ल in the e-text). Meaning given as "Vidula said:".
- 1.140, 1.142 "हिडिम्बोवाच" is Hidimbi (the sister, हिडिम्बा + उवाच), 1.141 "हिडिम्ब उवाच" her brother; meanings say so.
- 7.70.41 "स्वकेनाहमनीकेन … चेकितानमवारयम्": first-person "अहम्" in Sanjaya's narration of who faced whom is out of place; probably a speaker name (Ganguli-type renderings give Kritavarman, whose slot is otherwise missing). Meaning given as "Kritavarman … held back Chekitana" would be a guess; rendered as "I" in the text-faithful sense with a note here.
- 7.88.38 "यन्तारं ब्रह्माणं परिवर्जयन्": ब्रह्माणं is probably ब्राह्मणं (the brahmin Drona, avoided by Satyaki); rendered as "avoiding the brahmin".
