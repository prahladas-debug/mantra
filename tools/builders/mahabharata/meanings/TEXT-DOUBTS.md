# Text doubts found while writing meanings (check against a printed critical edition)

- 5.132, speaker line "विदुरोवाच": the adhyayas around it are Vidula's speech to her son (131, 133, 134 read "मातोवाच" / "पुत्र उवाच"), so this is probably "विदुलोवाच" (र for ल in the e-text). Meaning given as "Vidula said:".
- 1.140, 1.142 "हिडिम्बोवाच" is Hidimbi (the sister, हिडिम्बा + उवाच), 1.141 "हिडिम्ब उवाच" her brother; meanings say so.
