# Text doubts found while writing meanings (check against a printed critical edition)

- 5.132, speaker line "विदुरोवाच": the adhyayas around it are Vidula's speech to her son (131, 133, 134 read "मातोवाच" / "पुत्र उवाच"), so this is probably "विदुलोवाच" (र for ल in the e-text). Meaning given as "Vidula said:".
- 1.140, 1.142 "हिडिम्बोवाच" is Hidimbi (the sister, हिडिम्बा + उवाच), 1.141 "हिडिम्ब उवाच" her brother; meanings say so.
- 7.70.41 "स्वकेनाहमनीकेन … चेकितानमवारयम्": first-person "अहम्" in Sanjaya's narration of who faced whom is out of place; probably a speaker name (Ganguli-type renderings give Kritavarman, whose slot is otherwise missing). Meaning given as "Kritavarman … held back Chekitana" would be a guess; rendered as "I" in the text-faithful sense with a note here.
- 7.88.38 "यन्तारं ब्रह्माणं परिवर्जयन्": ब्रह्माणं is probably ब्राह्मणं (the brahmin Drona, avoided by Satyaki); rendered as "avoiding the brahmin".
- 8.10.20 "प्राहिणोत्तव पुत्राय": the spear is hurled by Chitra (a son of Dhritarashtra) at Prativindhya (a Pandava son), so "तव पुत्राय" ("to your son") does not fit; meaning given as "at him" (Prativindhya).
- 7.150.31 "प्रादुश्चक्रे महामायां राक्षसः पाण्डुनन्दनः": "पाण्डुनन्दनः" for Ghatotkacha (Bhima's son) looks like a slip (perhaps "पाण्डुनन्दनम्"/"पाण्डवात्मजः"); rendered "the rakshasa, delight of the Pandavas".
- 7.141.55 "यानं नन्दकस्य महात्मनः": Duryodhana leaps onto the chariot of "Nandaka", an otherwise unknown warrior (Nandaka is Vishnu's sword); rendered literally as "the chariot of the great-souled Nandaka".
- 9.4.49 "आकाशे विद्रुमे पुण्ये प्रस्थे हिमवतः शुभे": "आकाशे विद्रुमे" is unintelligible in the context (a halt on a Himalayan slope at the Sarasvati); rendered as "on a holy, auspicious Himalayan slope".
- 9.7.8 "न न एकेन योद्धव्यं": the doubled "न न" looks like a slip for "नो"/"नो न"; rendered as "none of us is to fight the Pandavas alone".
- 9.37.35 "स वि शाकरसं दृष्ट्वा": "स वि" looks like a slip for "स हि"/"स तु"; rendered as "Seeing that juice, he ...".
- 9.37.53 "स हि पुत्रः सजन्यायामुत्पन्नो मातरिश्वना": "सजन्यायाम्" is unclear (perhaps a woman's name or a slip for "सुकन्यायाम्"); rendered literally as "born by Sajanya to Matarishvan".
- 8.40.84 "वर्जयित्वा रणे याहि सूतपुत्रं महारथम्": reads "go, avoiding Karna" right after 8.40.83 asks Krishna to go where Karna is; rendered literally ("go, passing by the charioteer's son ... fatigue troubles me, or as it pleases you").
- 8.40.130 "वसुषेणं च पाञ्चालः कृत्स्नेन व्यधमद्रणे": case endings look swapped (the Panchala "wrecking" Vasushena); rendered by the evident sense, "Karna the Panchalas entirely".
- 8.42.53 "तं तु हित्वा हतं वीरं सारथिः": "हित्वा हतं" (leaving, slain) is awkward for a charioteer rescuing his master; rendered by the evident sense, "carried that hero, thought slain, out of the battlefield".
- 12.74.15 "यदा ब्रह्मा ब्रह्मणि त्राणमिच्छेत्": "ब्रह्मा" looks like a slip for "ब्रह्म"/"ब्राह्मणः"; rendered by the evident sense, a fallen non-celibate seeking protection in Brahmanahood.
- 9.26.25 "मम ह्येतदशक्तं वै वाजिवृन्दमरिंदम": "मम" looks like a slip for "तस्य"/"तव" (it is the enemy's horses that cannot bear the bowstring's twang); rendered as "This herd of horses cannot bear the twang of the bowstring".
- 9.28.83 "असंभावितवांश्चापि राजदारान्पुरं प्रति": "असंभावितवान्" looks like a slip for "संभावितवान्"/"अन्वगमत्"; rendered as "escorted the royal women toward the city".
