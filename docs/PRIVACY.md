# Privacy Policy: Mantra Sangraha

_Last updated: November 2026_

Mantra Sangraha is an offline book of mantras, stotras and pārāyaṇa texts.

- **No personal data is collected.** The app has no account, no analytics, no
  advertising and no tracking.
- **Everything stays on your device.** Your settings, favourites, bookmarks,
  reading positions, pārāyaṇa plans and japa counts are stored only on your
  phone, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **No internet connection is needed.** The pañcāṅga is calculated on the
  device. The city you choose for it never leaves your phone.
- **Notifications** are local reminders that you set for your pārāyaṇa plans.
  They are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server.
- **Optional links.** "Share" uses your phone's share sheet. "Report a
  correction" opens a web page (a GitHub issue form) in your browser, and only
  sends what you choose to submit there.
